# Orbit Navigator governance

## Project identity

Orbit Navigator is published under the public project identity **Orbit Nav
Pub**.

The project maintainer is **Paradox (aka TheArranger or TheCodeArranger)**.
This is the individual name to use when a maintainer or human project contact
must be identified. Product, package, installer, and general publisher fields
use Orbit Nav Pub.

There are currently no documented external contributors. Future contributors
will be identified by the public version-control history and release notes;
they do not automatically become publishers, trademark owners, or signing-key
custodians.

## Decisions and review

Paradox presently maintains the project and coordinates releases. Changes are
reviewed through the public repository workflow once that repository exists.
Security reports follow `SECURITY.md`; ordinary support and defects follow
`SUPPORT.md`.

Code-signing enrollment may require separately assigned author, reviewer, and
release-approver roles. Those roles and their access controls must be recorded
before a SignPath Foundation application is treated as ready. No signing key
or service credential belongs in this repository.
